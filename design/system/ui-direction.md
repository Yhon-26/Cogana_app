# Cogana UI Direction

Figma page: 104:2
Documentation frame: 104:3

## North star

Cogana brings the trust and familiarity of the traditional local market into
a clear, fast, and easy-to-buy digital experience.

The system may use established grocery-commerce patterns for search,
navigation, hierarchy, and conversion. It must not copy another retailer's
visual identity.

## Direction words

- Cercano
- Confiable
- Alimentario
- Popular
- Moderno
- Fácil de comprar

`Popular` means familiar, accessible, and understandable. It does not mean
low-quality or visually careless.

## Visual methods

- Functional minimalism
- Card-based design
- Flat Design 2.0 / semi-flat design
- Medium information density for customer-commerce screens
- Higher, controlled density for owner-administration screens

## Commercial hierarchy

1. Product recognition and imagery
2. Price and promotion
3. Presentation and availability
4. Primary purchase action
5. Secondary actions

Real catalog photography must replace provisional product illustrations in
production screens. The current vector media remains valid only as structured
placeholder content.

## Customer experience

- Dominant imagery and visible categories
- Large prices and explicit promotions
- Everyday, action-oriented language
- Comfortable mobile spacing
- One primary decision per content block

## Owner experience

- More compact and scannable information
- Contextual statuses, alerts, and metrics
- Fast access to frequent actions
- Search and filters before decoration
- The same accessibility and safe touch-target rules

## Guardrails

Use large-commerce products as UX references, not as identity references.
Avoid luxury positioning, excessive artisanal styling, glassmorphism, heavy
gradients, large decorative shadows, and whitespace that reduces useful
commercial information.

## Decision rule

If an option looks more sophisticated but makes products harder to find,
compare, or buy, Cogana chooses the clearer option.
